Three flows. One clear picture.

Every ringgit, dollar, and rupiah of your gaji has a job — covering loans, fixed commitments, and your goals. DuitPlan models it the way you actually think about it.

Hari Gaji, automatic

On payday, we log your salary, deduct your loan auto-debits, and roll your fixed commitments into envelopes. You see what's left to spend.

Loans & financing, tracked

Car loan, house financing, credit card — all in one place with debt-to-income, payoff timeline, and Hari Gaji auto-deduction reconciled.

Goals, locked away

Umrah, emergency fund, kids' school — earmark money before you can spend it. Out of sight, on track.

How it works

1

Import a statement

Drop in a BIBD or Baiduri PDF. We extract the transactions and categorise them automatically — no bank connection needed.

2

Set your gaji & commitments

Tell us your monthly salary, payday, and fixed bills. We build your envelope plan in seconds.

3

Plan, track, repeat

Every Hari Gaji, the cycle resets. Your loans get paid, your goals get fed, and you know exactly what's safe to spend.
