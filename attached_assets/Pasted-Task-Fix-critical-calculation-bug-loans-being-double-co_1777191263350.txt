Task: Fix critical calculation bug — loans being double-counted as both Commitments AND Debts
Severity: LAUNCH BLOCKER. This bug corrupts the headline "Remaining this month" number on every user's Dashboard. It must ship before any soft-launch.

The bug
Step 3 (Monthly fixed commitments) and Step 5 (Loans & financing) of onboarding both expose the same loan tiles: Car Loan, Personal Loan, House Financing, Credit Card. They write to two different tables (commitments and debts). The Dashboard's "Fixed Commitments" KPI sums the commitments table; the "Remaining this month" calculation subtracts both commitments AND debt repayments independently. Result: any loan declared in both gets subtracted twice from Remaining.
Reproduction:

Sign up as a new user
In onboarding Step 3, click the "Car Loan" tile and enter BND 450
Continue to Step 5, click the "Car Loan" tile and enter Monthly Payment BND 450 + Outstanding Balance BND 18,000
Finish onboarding and go to the Dashboard

Expected: Fixed Commitments shows BND 450 (or 0, depending on model)
Actual: Fixed Commitments shows BND 900 — same Car Loan counted twice
The Commitments page also shows two separate "Car Loan" entries, both BND 450.

Root cause
The data model conflates two distinct concepts:

Commitments = recurring bills owed to vendors/services (rent, utilities, subscriptions, family support)
Debts = money owed to lenders that has a balance and a payoff date (car loan, mortgage, credit card, personal loan)

Loans got accidentally exposed in both places during onboarding, and the calculation logic on Dashboard counts each table independently.
